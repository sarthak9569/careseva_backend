## 1. Introduction & Scope
Softkrest Infotech ("We", "Us", "Our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and healthcare data. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, processed, stored, and shared when you use the CareSeva Mobile Application and Web Portal.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect data strictly necessary to facilitate OPD token bookings and healthcare management:

1. **Personal Information:** Name, mobile phone number, age, gender, date of birth, and home address.
2. **Appointment & OPD Data:** Selected hospital, department, treating doctor, appointment date, assigned OPD token number, time slot, and consultation payment status.
3. **Medical Records (Where Uploaded):** Prescriptions, diagnostic reports, and medical history uploaded by you or issued by treating healthcare providers.
4. **Technical & Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, push notification tokens, IP address, and application logs.

---

## 3. Purpose of Processing
Your data is processed for the following lawful purposes:
* Authenticating mobile logins via OTP.
* Enrolling you in live hospital OPD queues and providing real-time token tracking.
* Transmitting appointment booking details to selected hospitals and doctors.
* Generating payment invoices and processing refunds via authorized payment gateways.
* Sending critical notifications (e.g., "Doctor is calling your token").

---

## 4. Strict Data Isolation & Security Architecture
* **Patient Data Isolation:** CareSeva employs multi-tenant backend architecture with JSON Web Token (JWT) authorization. Your personal profile and appointment history are strictly isolated; no other patient can view your records.
* **Encryption:** Data transmitted between the CareSeva mobile application and backend servers is encrypted using standard **TLS 1.3 / HTTPS** protocols.

---

## 5. Controlled Third-Party Data Sharing
Softkrest Infotech **DOES NOT SELL, RENT, OR TRADE YOUR PERSONAL OR HEALTH DATA TO ADVERTISERS OR MARKETERS**.

Your data is shared exclusively with:
1. **Target Hospitals & Doctors:** The specific hospital reception desk and treating doctor where you book an OPD token.
2. **Payment Service Providers:** Authorized gateways (e.g., Razorpay) for processing consultation fees.
3. **Communication Infrastructure Providers:** SMS/WhatsApp vendors (e.g., Fast2SMS/Twilio) to dispatch login OTPs and token updates.
4. **Cloud Infrastructure:** Secure MongoDB Atlas and Railway cloud database servers.

---

## 6. Your Rights under the DPDP Act 2023
As a Data Principal, you possess the following statutory rights:
* **Right to Access:** Request a summary of your personal data processed by CareSeva.
* **Right to Correction:** Request correction or updating of inaccurate personal profile data.
* **Right to Consent Withdrawal:** Withdraw consent for data processing at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer.
* **Right to Account Erasure:** Request complete deletion of your account and personal profile (subject to statutory medical record retention rules required for hospitals under Indian medical laws).

---

## 7. Data Retention
Personal account profile data is retained as long as your account remains active. OPD appointment and queue logs are retained in accordance with applicable healthcare record-keeping guidelines.

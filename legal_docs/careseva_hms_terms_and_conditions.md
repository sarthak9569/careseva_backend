## 1. Scope of Agreement & Software Licensing
This Business-to-Business (B2B) Software-as-a-Service Agreement governs access to and use of the **CareSeva Hospital Management System (HMS)** web portal and administrative interfaces. 

Softkrest Infotech grants registered hospitals, clinics, administrators, doctors, receptionists, and authorized healthcare staff ("Healthcare Partner") a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to access the CareSeva HMS platform for outpatient department (OPD) queue management, patient registration, IPD admissions, and consultation workflow automation.

## 2. Hospital Clinical & Operational Responsibilities
1. **Medical Qualifications:** Healthcare Partner warrants that all doctors and clinical staff onboarded onto CareSeva HMS hold valid, active medical licenses issued by the National Medical Commission (NMC) or relevant State Medical Councils.
2. **Clinical Autonomy & Liability:** Healthcare Partner assumes **exclusive clinical, medical, legal, and regulatory liability** for all patient diagnoses, OPD consultations, prescriptions, treatments, IPD admissions, and medical advice provided through or recorded in CareSeva HMS.
3. **Data Accuracy:** Healthcare Partner is solely responsible for verifying patient identity during walk-in registrations and maintaining accurate doctor schedules, consultation fees, and department listings.

## 3. Account Credential Security & Staff Offboarding
* **Individual Accounts:** Hospital staff members must use individual, authenticated login credentials. Sharing administrative or receptionist accounts across multiple staff members is strictly prohibited.
* **Immediate Revocation:** Healthcare Partner must immediately deactivate or revoke access credentials for any doctor, receptionist, or staff member who resigns, is terminated, or no longer requires system access.

---

## 4. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
CareSeva HMS enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
* **Hospital Administrator:** Full operational control, doctor management, department setup, and dashboard analytics.
* **Doctor Portal:** Access restricted to active OPD queues, patient consultation calls, digital prescriptions, and clinical history for assigned patients.
* **Reception Desk:** Access restricted to patient walk-in registration, fee collection, token issuance, and emergency IPD admissions.

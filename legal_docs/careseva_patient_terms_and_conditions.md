## 6. OPD Token Allocation & Live Queue Status
* **Token Validity:** OPD tokens issued through CareSeva represent an assigned queue order for a specific doctor on a specific date.
* **Dynamic Waiting Times:** Estimated wait times and live queue positions are dynamic calculations based on real-time OPD flow. Hospitals may experience delays due to emergency patient admissions or unexpected surgical procedures.
* **Token Modifications:** Hospitals reserve the right to call tokens out of sequential order in cases of clinical emergency or doctor unavailability.
